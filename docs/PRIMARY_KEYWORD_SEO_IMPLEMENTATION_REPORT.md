## 3. Dedicated Landing Page: `/budget-stay-isha/`

To capture high-volume transactional and informational search queries without diluting the core homestay page, a dedicated landing page was authored at `budget-stay-isha/index.html`.

### Key Features:
1. **Schema.org `@graph` Architecture:**
   - Unified `LodgingBusiness` entity with canonical `@id: https://www.nishahomestay.com/#business`.
   - Connected `WebPage` with `about` and `mainEntity` referencing the business.
   - Connected `BreadcrumbList` (`Home` → `Budget Stay Near Isha Yoga Center`).
   - Connected `FAQPage` featuring 4 synchronized questions with rich snippet targeting.
2. **Transparent Value Comparison Table:**
   - Compares Nisha Home Stay vs. Coimbatore City Hotels vs. Ashram Shared Dormitories.
   - Highlights 1.8 km distance vs. 30 km city commute, private 1BHK privacy vs. dormitory bunk beds, and transparent ₹1,500 base rate.
3. **Structured Tariff Grid:**
   - 1 Guest: ₹1,500 / 24 hrs
   - 2 Guests: ₹2,000 / 24 hrs
   - 3 Guests: ₹2,500 / 24 hrs
   - 4-5 Guests: ₹3,000 / 24 hrs
   - 6-7 Guests: ₹3,500 / 24 hrs
4. **Direct Booking CTA Actions:**
   - Primary Phone link: `tel:+916381397636` with GA4 tracking attribute `data-track="hero_call"`.
   - Secondary WhatsApp link: `#booking` directing to interactive booking form.
5. **Exact Distance Adherence:**
   - Isha Yoga Center: Approximately 1.8 km (8 minutes driving).
   - Adiyogi Shiva Statue: Approximately 2.2 km (10 minutes driving).
   - Velliangiri Foothills: Approximately 5 km.
